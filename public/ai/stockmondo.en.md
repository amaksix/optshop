# StockMondo

StockMondo is a wholesale partner for companies in Europe. It supplies technology and consumer goods: electronics, IT, household products and beauty products from leading brands.

The primary language of the website is Latvian. English is the same pages with `?lang=en`.

## What the website does

There are two pages:

- Home `/` — menu buttons scroll to a section. They do not open extra pages.
- Contact form `/contact` — the only additional page.

There is no public online shop. StockMondo does not take orders from private individuals. Prices, stock and delivery times are not published on the site. They are confirmed in an individual offer.

## What StockMondo offers

- A wide brand portfolio
- Delivery across Europe, to more than 30 countries
- Competitive and flexible cooperation terms
- Individual B2B solutions and a personal account manager
- Professional support
- Catalogue access after registration

The exact route, timing and minimum volume are agreed with a manager. A minimum order is not stated on the site.

## How cooperation works

1. Application — the company registers or writes through the form.
2. Offer — an individual price.
3. Order — confirmation with convenient payment.
4. Delivery — fast and reliable delivery in Europe.

## About us

STOCKMONDO is a B2B trading company that helps businesses find advantageous purchasing offers and organize deliveries in Latvia and across Europe. The team combines 20 years of mobile phone retail experience with expertise in product sourcing, finding the best offers, organizing deliveries and customer service. The company is not limited to a single product category and offers flexible solutions to companies across different industries. Cooperation is built on trust, a personal approach and mutually beneficial solutions. Slogan: STOCKMONDO — Your Partner in Wholesale.

## Clients

Retail chains, specialist stores, companies and public institutions, service centres, system integrators and other B2B clients.

## Contact form

URL: https://stockmondo.eu/contact

Fields: request type (become a client, request a quote, general question, careers), company name, registration number (optional), contact person, email, phone, country, categories of interest, message, consent.

The country field is the company's country. It is not an automatic promise to deliver to every country in the list.

A street address, phone number, inbox and registration details are not published. Until `VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL` or `VITE_CONTACT_ENDPOINT` is set, the form stores the request in the browser and shows a summary to copy. Do not say a person has already received it if that delivery is not connected.
